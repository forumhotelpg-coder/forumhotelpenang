Frequently asked questions

What is Forum Club?

Forum Club is Forum Hotel Penang's free membership programme. Members enjoy special rates, free night rewards and upgrades when they book direct.

How do I join?

Fill in the form on this page and tap "Join via WhatsApp". Our team will reply with your member number and welcome voucher.

What counts as a direct night?

A night booked directly with Forum Hotel through our website, WhatsApp, phone or walk-in. Bookings through Agoda, Booking.com, Trip.com or other booking sites, and group bookings, do not count.

How do I move up a tier?

Stay 5 direct nights within 12 months to become Silver, and 10 or more to become Gold. Our team keeps track of your nights and lets you know on WhatsApp.

How do I use my member benefits?

Enter your member number in the booking form, or tell us when you book on WhatsApp or at the front desk.

Can I transfer my membership or vouchers?

No. Membership, nights and vouchers are personal and cannot be transferred or exchanged for cash.

Does membership include breakfast?

Breakfast is not available at the moment, but there are many local breakfast spots within walking distance of the hotel.

Good to know

Property policies

Check-in & check-out

Check-in from 3:00 pm · Check-out by 12:00 pm.

Security deposit

We collect a RM 100 security deposit per room, in cash only. It is refunded upon check-out.

Tourism tax & heritage fee

Foreign (non-Malaysian) guests pay a tourism tax of RM 10 per night. A heritage fee of RM 2 per night applies to all guests, Malaysian and non-Malaysian.

Parking

Parking spaces at the property are limited and subject to availability.

Standard King Room

Please note this room type has no view (corridor view).

Group bookings

When booking more than 5 rooms, different policies and additional supplements may apply.

No smoking

Smoking is not allowed in the rooms or anywhere inside the hotel. A RM 200 fine or cleaning fee will be charged for smoking in a room.

No pets

Pets are not allowed at the hotel.

Explore Penang

Famous places near the hotel

Stay in the centre of George Town and Penang's best sights are close by. Ask our front desk for directions, Grab or tour tips.

≈ 5 min walk
Next to our hotel

榴莲树树 Durian shop

Fresh Penang durians at 27 Jalan Sri Bahari — rated 5.0★ on Google. Open until 10 pm.

≈ 10 min walk
Next to our hotel

Pasar Chowrasta

One of George Town's oldest markets on Penang Road — local snacks, nutmeg, pickled fruits and Penang favourites.

≈ 10 min walk
Heritage

Cheong Fatt Tze – The Blue Mansion

The famous indigo-blue 19th-century Chinese courtyard mansion, with daily guided tours.

≈ 20 min walk
Street art

George Town Street Art

Hunt for the famous murals and iron-rod sculptures along Armenian Street and nearby lanes.

≈ 20 min walk
Heritage

Kapitan Keling Mosque

A grand 19th-century mosque with golden domes, in the heart of the UNESCO heritage zone.

≈ 10 min drive
Culture

Chew Jetty

A wooden stilt village over the sea, home to the Chew clan for generations.

≈ 20 min drive
Temple

Kek Lok Si Temple

One of Southeast Asia's largest Buddhist temples, with a towering pagoda and giant Kuan Yin statue.

≈ 20 min drive
Nature

Penang Hill

Ride the funicular up for cool air and panoramic views over George Town.

≈ 10 min drive
Temple

Dhammikarama Burmese Temple

Penang's first Burmese Buddhist temple, known for its ornate halls and statues.

≈ 10 min drive
Food & shopping

Gurney Drive

Seafront promenade with famous hawker food, plus Gurney Plaza and Gurney Paragon malls.
